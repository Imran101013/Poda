Conference 2025 Booklet & Resolution

6 November 2025 Annual Rural Women Leadership Training Conference

PODA has published the booklet and resolution from the 18th Annual Rural Women Leadership Training Conference, held 15–17 October 2025 in Islamabad. The conference brought together over 1,500 rural women leaders from 100 districts across Pakistan, commemorating International Day of Rural Women, World Food Day, and World Day for Poverty Eradication.

The final day of the conference saw the adoption of an 18-point resolution, produced by working groups across four thematic areas and carrying the recommendations of all 1,500+ participants into a formal policy document:

  • Legal Identity & Protection — CNIC enrollment and the minimum marriage age of 18.
  • Education & Economic Empowerment — rural girls' schools and women as farmers.
  • Disaster & Climate Response — women's representation in disaster management bodies.
  • Workplace & Governance — 50% local government seats for women.

The booklet documents all three days of the conference — from the opening keynotes on constitutional rights and legal identity, through the second day's policy working groups, to the closing ceremony where the resolution was adopted. Copies of the booklet and resolution are available on request; see the full day-by-day record on the Annual Conference page.
