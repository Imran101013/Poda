Who we are

Rights-Based Work, Rooted in Rural Pakistan

Potohar Organization for Development Advocacy (PODA) is a women's rights NGO working for the promotion and protection of human rights in rural areas of Pakistan — through evidence-based research, rights-based programming and impact-oriented advocacy.

Since 2003, our field teams have worked district by district on climate change education, women's rights advocacy and constitutional rights education, led today by Executive Director Sameena Nazir.

"A democratic society based on gender equity and economic justice, where all people can enjoy their Right to Development."

— PODA Vision Statement

Our purpose

What We Stand For, and the Change We Promise

PODA is a registered non-profit whose mission is to make a difference in the lives of rural women, youth and minorities — primarily through education, legal literacy and rights-based advocacy. We work to transform mind, body and soul, and to educate and equip citizens of Pakistan through scholarships, training and grassroots organizing.
